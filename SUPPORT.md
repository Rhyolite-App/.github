# Getting Help with Rhyolite

## Built-in Help

Press **F1** inside Rhyolite on macOS, Windows, or Linux (or tap the **?** help button in the sidebar) to open the searchable Help tab. It covers every feature, search operator, Vim motion, CLI flag, MCP setup, and your live keyboard shortcuts.

## Support & Documentation

- **Support page:** [https://rhyolite.app/support](https://rhyolite.app/support)
- **Bug reports & feature requests:** [Rhyolite-App/releases Issues](https://github.com/Rhyolite-App/releases/issues/new/choose)
- **Downloads & release notes:** [Rhyolite-App/releases](https://github.com/Rhyolite-App/releases/releases/latest)
- **Email support:** [support@rhyolite.app](mailto:support@rhyolite.app) — let us know which device and OS version you use and what you were doing.
- **iPhone & iPad TestFlight beta:** take a screenshot in the app and choose **Share Beta Feedback** in TestFlight, or email [support@rhyolite.app](mailto:support@rhyolite.app).

## Security Vulnerabilities

Please do **not** open public issues for security bugs. Follow our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) and email **[support@rhyolite.app](mailto:support@rhyolite.app)**.
